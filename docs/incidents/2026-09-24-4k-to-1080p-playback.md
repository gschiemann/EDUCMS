# A 4K video sent to a 1080p player (2026-09-24)

**Date:** 2026-09-24

## What happened

(2026-09-24 4K-to-1080p playback incident). A 1080p Android player received a 4K H.264 asset, briefly showed Content Unavailable, then reported severe dropped frames; a manually prepared 1080p copy played smoothly.

## Root cause

A fresh playlist publish sent 4K again because optimization was upload-only and the manifest had no screen-specific file selection.

## The rule it produced

`apps/web/src/app/player/CLAUDE.md` → rule 16, "Screen-sized publication is a staged delivery, not a database boolean" (`schedules/media-publication.service.ts` queues the 1080p rendition and keeps the old schedule active until it exists; `screens/video-rendition.ts` picks URL, hash and size as one unit).
