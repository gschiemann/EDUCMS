## Lens: competitive-parity

VenueOS's Phase-1 App Library matches the competitor SPINE well: a searchable card grid + category filter, a per-app config form with a genuinely-truthful WidgetPreview (same component the canvas renders — OptiSigns/ScreenCloud/Yodeck all have live preview too, so this is table-stakes we hit), an "Add to canvas" confirm, and honest friction-tier badges (OptiSigns/ScreenCloud actively HIDE the publish/login burden until after you pick — our badge-first-and-sort-by-friction is already a genuine edge worth protecting). But two things make us NOT credible today and three assets we already own are sitting unused.

MUST-MATCH GAPS (we lose without these): (1) Every competitor ships PREBUILT FINISHED LAYOUTS per app — Rise Vision 560+, Yodeck 800+, OptiSigns 160+ — where "add YouTube" or "add Slides" drops a designed board, not a bare rectangle. Our AppConfigForm's `handleConfirm` (AppConfigForm.tsx:56) drops ONE naked zone onto the canvas — the operator then has to design around it. This is the single biggest parity gap and we uniquely have the AI Designer to beat it, not just match it. (2) OptiSigns offers "Sign in with Google" OAuth for Google Slides/Sheets/Calendar so PRIVATE decks work with zero manual publish-to-web; our Slides/Sheets/Canva/Calendar apps punt the entire publish-to-web / .ics-secret-address burden onto the operator via a yellow setupNote (app-registry.ts:216,269,438) — a non-IT operator will paste an /edit link, hit a Google sign-in wall inside the iframe, and think we're broken. (3) News/RSS (app-registry.ts:404) and Calendar (app-registry.ts:444) are wired to widgets that DON'T actually fetch — the RSS widget renders 5 hardcoded placeholder headlines and CalendarWidget never parses the ICS feed. Shipping these as live-looking tiles is exactly the "silent-break app" anti-pattern the synthesis (§7) says never to copy from competitors.

BEAT-THEM MOVES we already own but haven't wired: the AI Concierge (POST /integrations/discover + /describe already live and returning ranked provider candidates), the AI Designer (finished-board-per-app generation), and an embeddability HEAD-check that no rival ships. Every recommendation below REMOVES friction (auto-detect, one-click, sensible default) rather than adding a knob — per Greg's "no more settings" rule.

### [P0] Apps drop a NAKED zone, not a finished board — every competitor ships prebuilt layouts per app; we own the AI Designer to beat them and don't use it  _(effort L, REMOVES friction ✅)_
- **Today:** AppConfigForm.handleConfirm (apps/web/src/components/apps/AppConfigForm.tsx:56-61) does `addZone(built.widgetType)` + `updateZone(id, {defaultConfig})` — it drops ONE bare widget zone onto the canvas and returns. The operator now stares at a single un-styled rectangle and has to design the whole board around it. Meanwhile Rise Vision ships 560+ templates, Yodeck 800+, OptiSigns 160+ apps each with designed layouts, and OptiSigns' AI Designer generates a finished on-brand board 'in under 30 seconds.' The synthesis (00-SYNTHESIS.md §4.3) explicitly names this as THE way we beat OptiSigns: 'Finished-board templates per app, not bare widgets… 2-3 designer-grade layouts via our AI Designer with the app's data already placed.' We built the panel and skipped the differentiator.
- **World-class fix:** On the config form, below the live preview, show 2-3 finished-board thumbnails for that app ('YouTube + branded lower-third title', 'Slides full-bleed', 'Slides + clock + weather rail'). Clicking one adds a fully-designed multi-zone board with the operator's URL already placed — not a bare zone. Source these from the existing AI Designer (the same generate-candidates path BuilderShell already has a 'review' panel for) keyed by app id + tenant brand, cached. Keep the plain 'Add to canvas' as the fast path so it's a default-rich-plus-escape-hatch, not a new required step. REMOVES friction (operator gets a designed board in one click instead of designing from scratch).

(2) Roadmap the real win to match OptiSigns: 'Sign in with Google' OAuth (we already have Google OAuth plumbing per the OIDC work) so private decks/calendars work with no publish step at all. Both REMOVE friction — neither is a knob.

### [P0] News/RSS and Calendar apps are silent-break tiles — wired to widgets that render placeholders and never fetch the feed  _(effort L, REMOVES friction ✅)_

### [P1] No embeddability pre-flight on the Web Page / URL app — competitors don't ship this either, so it's a cheap credibility win  _(effort M, REMOVES friction ✅)_
- **Today:** The Web Page/URL app (app-registry.ts:281) builds a WEBPAGE zone from any pasted URL via ensureHttps (url-transforms.ts:141) with zero check that the site ALLOWS embedding. ~30-40% of major sites send X-Frame-Options:DENY or frame-ancestors CSP, so the operator pastes e.g. a bank/news/gov URL, sees a preview that may render locally, ships it, and the live screen shows a blank/refused frame. The live preview (AppConfigForm.tsx:84) can't reliably catch this because the builder runs on a different origin than the null-origin sandboxed player iframe. The synthesis §4.6 explicitly flags this as a 'guardrail competitors lack' and OptiSigns/ScreenCloud/Yodeck docs show no pre-flight — they let you add a URL and discover it's blocked on-screen.
- **World-class fix:** On URL entry (debounced), do a server-side HEAD request checking X-Frame-Options / Content-Security-Policy frame-ancestors and show an inline warning BEFORE Add: 'example.com blocks embedding — it won't display on screen. Try a screenshot/snapshot instead.' No new setting — just an honest inline check that stops the operator shipping a guaranteed-blank board. Pairs naturally with the Slides/Sheets preflight above (same server-side reachability check). REMOVES friction (catches the failure at add-time, not on the wall).
- **Files:** apps/web/src/components/apps/AppConfigForm.tsx, apps/web/src/components/apps/url-transforms.ts, apps/web/src/components/apps/app-registry.ts

Surfaces the app library from the Screens/Playlists flow too, not just deep inside the template editor. REMOVES friction (collapses the multi-step template detour for the single-app case) — no new setting, just a second destination for the same built config.

## Lens: concierge-autofill

The App Library ships as a well-built manual picker (19 tiles, honest friction tiers, live preview, one-click "Add to canvas") but is completely disconnected from the Integration Concierge — the single highest-leverage "effortless" mechanism VenueOS already owns. The backend `/api/v1/integrations/discover` + `/describe` endpoints are fully built, SSRF-safe, rate-limited, and RBAC-gated — yet a repo-wide grep of `apps/web/src` for `integrations/discover`/`integrations/describe` returns ZERO callers: nothing in the app, onboarding, or the Apps tab ever invokes them. So the operator opens Apps to a wall of empty tiles and must know their own YouTube link, Slides publish-URL, iCal address, RSS feed, etc. — the exact IT-consultant friction Greg wants removed ("the app almost does the work for them"). Worse, even the discovery service that DOES run only classifies provider *categories* (discovery.service.ts:691-706) — it throws the scraped `linkHrefs` (line 666) into a match haystack and discards the operator's actual handles, so it can't pre-fill a tile even conceptually today. The world-class version: when Apps opens (and once during onboarding right after the branding scrape that already fetches the homepage + og: tags), auto-detect the operator's OWN links and float pre-filled, badged tiles ("We found your Instagram", "We found your Google Slides") at the top of the grid — a suggestion, never a forced step. Add a capped Tier-1 AI one-line explainer on hover for unfamiliar tiles and a Tier-1 AI diagnostic ("here's why it's not showing + 3 steps") when a WEBPAGE/STREAMING app renders blank — both can reuse the exact bug-analyzer.service.ts platform-key/cap/audit plumbing that already ships. Every finding below REMOVES friction; none adds a setting. Findings ranked by leverage: the pre-fill wiring is P0 because it converts a "know your own URLs" chore into a "confirm what we found" tap.

### [P0] Discovery service can't actually pre-fill a tile — it classifies provider CATEGORIES and discards the operator's own handles/URLs  _(effort M, REMOVES friction ✅)_
- **Today:** IntegrationDiscoveryService.buildHaystack (discovery.service.ts:657-682) collects every a[href]/link[href] into `linkHrefs` (line 666) and og:/meta content, then scoreRules (691-706) only tests provider RegExps against the flattened haystack and returns a ProviderCandidate whose most-specific field is `matchedSignals: string[]` (e.g. 'Instagram link') — the ACTUAL profile URL (https://instagram.com/theirhandle) is never captured. The branding scraper (branding-scraper.service.ts) already parses og: tags and even has a social-glyph regex at line 677, proving the raw material is fetched and thrown away.
- **World-class fix:** Extend the discovery pass with a small `extractOwnLinks(html)` that harvests the operator\'s own destinations: footer/nav a[href] matching youtube.com|youtu.be, instagram.com, facebook.com, vimeo.com, twitch.tv, docs.google.com/presentation, calendar.google.com/*.ics, an RSS <link rel=alternate type=application/rss+xml>, and JSON-LD `sameAs[]`. Return them on a new `ProviderCandidate.detectedValue?: string` (and a top-level `ownLinks` map) so the Apps tab can map youtube→app 'youtube', slides→'google-slides', rss→'news-rss', ical→'calendar' and open each PRE-FILLED. This is the difference between 'we noticed you use X' (useless) and 'here\'s your board, one tap to add' (effortless). Keep it deterministic/keyless — no new AI cost, no new setting.

## Lens: config-form-ease

The AppConfigForm is a clean, generic, single-column form — but for a non-IT operator it is a "wall of inputs" wizard-in-disguise, not a guided flow, and its headline promise ("a truthful live preview, not a mockup that can drift") is false for the six WEBPAGE apps that need the most hand-holding.
