**On SSRF specifically:** an operator **cannot** point a webhook at `169.254.169.254` or an internal host. Three layers: IP-literal + scheme + port 80/443 rejection at create (`webhooks.service.ts:84-93`), DNS-name blocklist for `localhost`/`.local` (`:97-102`), and — the load-bearing one — a connect-time `lookup` pin re-resolved on **every attempt including retries**, which closes the DNS-rebind TOCTOU (`safe-fetch.ts:122-145`, wired at `:214`). ## Findings

**[P1] Two duplicate Resend senders never received the `SENT_UNVERIFIED` honesty fix — platform-ops alerts record a confident "SENT" for mail Resend silently drops** — `storage-watchdog.service.ts:166,175` and `efficiency-alerting.service.ts:166,179` vs `email.service.ts:424-428,498-507`. The `SENT_UNVERIFIED` fix landed in a single commit (`a995d72e`) that touched **only** `email.service.ts`; both copies still hardcode the `onboarding@resend.dev` fallback and stamp `status:'SENT'` on any 200. `grep -c "SENT_UNVERIFIED\|isDeliverable"` returns **0** for both files. The comment at `storage-watchdog.service.ts:126-128` even predicted this ("kept in sync by hand — extract a shared mailer if a third copy ever appears") and it has already drifted. Impact: on a deploy whose `EMAIL_FROM` is not domain-verified, a **storage outage** or **egress-cost anomaly** alert is dropped by Resend while `email_logs` says SENT — the exact failure mode the EmailService fix was written to kill, on the two channels that exist to tell you the platform is broken. Fix sketch: route both through `EmailService.#enqueue` (add a `sendPlatformAlert(kind, subject, body)` helper), deleting ~45 duplicated lines. — **NEW** (the generic "reports SENT" issue was KNOWN in `2026-07-16-launch-readiness/00-LEDGER.md:11`; the *residual drift in the two copies after the fix* is new).

**[P1] No retry or replay for any failed email — a transient Resend 5xx permanently loses a password-reset or invite** — `email.service.ts:142-159` marks the row FAILED and stops. Verified absent two ways: (1) `QUEUED` appears at only three write sites and zero read sites; (2) a full sweep of `@Cron`/`@Interval`/`setInterval` in `apps/api/src` lists nine workers (efficiency, storage-watchdog, sports clock, screen-wedge, SSE, time-sync, clever-sync, pos-sync, canary-promote) and **none** touches `email_logs`. Impact: asymmetric with webhooks, which get a lease-based retry worker for the *same* class of transient failure. A user whose reset mail hits a Resend blip must re-request and has no signal anything went wrong. Fix sketch: a small `EmailRetryWorker` reusing the webhook worker's `SKIP LOCKED` claim pattern, capped at 3 attempts, skipping `PASSWORD_RESET` rows older than the 1-hour token TTL. — **KNOWN** (`2026-07-03-overnight-fixes/09-email-audit.md:76-78`, filed as "ops gap, not scored"; still open 4 weeks later).

**[P1] Email has no operator-visible delivery surface anywhere in the product — the answer to "does an operator ever find out" is No** — `grep -rniIl "emailLog\|email_logs\|email-logs" apps/web/src` returns **zero files**; every hit is server-side. `EmailLog` also has **no `tenantId`** (`schema.prisma:665-679`), so it could not be tenant-scoped even if a page existed. Impact: a district admin who invites five staff and has a non-verified sending domain sees five apparent successes and no way to discover the failures; diagnosis requires Railway logs or direct DB access. Contrast — webhooks *do* surface `lastDeliveryStatus`/`lastDeliveryError`/`lastDeliveryAt` per row (`settings/developer/page.tsx:922-955`), and integrations-health *does* surface the DEGRADED sender state (`integrations-health.controller.ts:698-719`). Email delivery itself is the blind spot. Fix sketch: a SUPER_ADMIN `/super/email-logs` table (kind, to, status, error, sentAt) with a resend button; add `tenantId` if it should ever be district-visible. — **KNOWN** (same 07-03 ref).

**[P2] Settings still advertises the shipped webhook feature as unbuilt** — `apps/web/src/app/[schoolId]/settings/page.tsx:402` reads "REST tokens + webhooks **(coming next release)**", but webhooks are fully shipped with CRUD, HMAC signing, SSRF pinning, durable retry and a status UI. A reverse-costume: the operator is told a real, differentiating, life-safety-relevant capability doesn't exist. Fix: one-line copy change. — **KNOWN** (`2026-07-12-world-class-fullapp-audit/03-catalog-business-findings.md:65`; unfixed ~3 weeks).

**[P2] Three `NotificationKind` members have zero emitters, including `EMERGENCY_TRIGGERED` — the bell renders an emergency icon that can never fire** — `notifications.service.ts:7-17` declares six kinds; an emitter sweep gives `SCREEN_OFFLINE`=2, `INFO`=4, `INFRA_EVENT`=3, and **`EMERGENCY_TRIGGERED`=0, `SYNC_FAILED`=0, `INVITE_ACCEPTED`=0**. Confirmed structurally: the emergency module imports neither `NotificationsService` nor `EmailService`. Yet `NotificationsBell.tsx:17` ships a dedicated red `ShieldAlert` icon for `EMERGENCY_TRIGGERED`. Impact: an admin who wasn't the trigger-er gets **no in-app record** that a lockdown fired — only the AuditLog (which they must go look for). For a life-safety product where a delegated `canTriggerPanic` staffer can fire district-wide, that is a real forensic/awareness gap, not just dead code. Fix sketch: emit a tenant-wide `EMERGENCY_TRIGGERED` notification alongside the existing AuditLog write in `emergency.controller.ts` (near the webhook dispatch at `:611`), and one on all-clear near `:788`. — **NEW**.

**[P2] "PagerDuty bridge" is a UI placeholder that implies drop-in compatibility it doesn't have** — `settings/developer/page.tsx:846` uses `placeholder="e.g. PagerDuty bridge"`. The outbound envelope is `{tenantId, event, timestamp, data}` with `X-VenueOS-Signature` (`webhook-dispatch.service.ts:119-122, 213-224`); PagerDuty Events API v2 requires `{routing_key, event_action, payload:{summary, severity, source}}`. Pointing a webhook straight at PagerDuty yields a 400 the operator will read as "our webhook is broken." Fix sketch: either change the placeholder (30-second fix), or ship a `format: 'pagerduty' | 'slack' | 'generic'` field that reshapes the body — Slack/Teams incoming webhooks would work with the same switch and would convert two N-A bullets into REAL. — **NEW**.

**[P2] No dunning / past-due email to the customer** — verified two ways: no email-related symbol in `apps/api/src/billing/*.ts`, and neither `apps/api/src/billing` nor `apps/api/src/license` imports `EmailService`. `invoice.payment_failed` flips the License to PAST_DUE with an audit row (`stripe.service.ts:400-406`) but notifies nobody. Partially mitigated — Stripe sends its own dunning mail when enabled in the dashboard, and the state is visible in-app. Worth an explicit owner decision rather than a silent gap. — **NEW**.

**[P2] No boot-time warning when `RESEND_API_KEY` is set but `EMAIL_FROM` is the shared sender** — absent from `main.ts`, `app.module.ts`, `required-secret.ts`. Now well-mitigated by the integrations-health DEGRADED row and `SENT_UNVERIFIED`, so lower severity than when first raised, but it remains the cheapest guard against the most common "no emails arrive" support spiral. — **KNOWN** (`2026-06-09-prelaunch-final-audit/sonnet-baseline/06-comms-publicalert.md:169`).

**[P2] Webhook signing secrets are stored plaintext-recoverable** — `webhooks.service.ts:117-129` writes `signingSecret` to `TenantWebhook` unencrypted. It must be recoverable to sign, so hashing is not an option, but the tenant AI BYOK key next door uses `aiKeyEncrypted` (`integrations-health.controller.ts:328`) — the encryption helper already exists. A DB-read compromise currently yields the ability to forge `emergency.triggered` payloads to a district's ops tooling. Fix: reuse the BYOK envelope encryption. — **NEW**.

## Strategic note for the V2 safety pivot

The load-bearing observation across both sections: **VenueOS today can alert screens, and nothing else.** A lockdown fans out over signed Redis pub/sub to displays, writes an immutable audit row, and optionally POSTs a webhook — but no human is texted, paged, pushed, or emailed by the platform itself. Every §13 bullet (CAP, IPAWS, Raptor, RapidSOS, PA/IP-speaker) is unbuilt, and §9's human channels (Twilio, push, Slack/Teams, PagerDuty) are unbuilt.

That is a coherent, honestly-communicated V1 scope, and the honesty is genuinely above industry norm — `COMING_SOON` rows are hidden from ordinary operators rather than dressed as DEGRADED (`integrations-health.controller.ts:190, 703-708`), the EULA affirmatively disclaims IPAWS/CAP/WEA/EAS/PA/NFPA-72/UL-2572 (`eula/page.tsx:110-116`), and there is a capability truth-gate test that would **fail CI** on a marketing claim about IPAWS (`capability-registry.spec.ts:63-67`). No §9/§13 costume found: no dropdown, toggle, or settings field offers a channel with no transport behind it.

Two owner decisions follow, neither an engineering task:

1. **IPAWS outbound origination** requires FEMA COG authorization plus IPAWS-OPEN certification — a legal/procurement track measured in months. Decide as a business commitment before scoping code.
2. **The cheapest credible step up the safety ladder is the generic webhook you already ship.** Adding a `format` switch (F7) turns Slack, Teams, and PagerDuty from N-A into working escalation on top of transport that is already signed, SSRF-pinned, durably retried and multi-replica-safe. Far better return than a native Twilio build, and it makes `emergency.triggered` reach humans.

Also worth expanding: `WebhooksService.ALLOWED_EVENTS` is still only `emergency.triggered` / `emergency.cleared` (`webhooks.service.ts:30-33`) while `screen.online`/`screen.offline` are already detected by the offline scanner — the comment at `:17-21` names them as future work and the detection exists today.
