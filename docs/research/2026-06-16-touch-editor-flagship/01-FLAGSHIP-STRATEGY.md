# Touch/Template Editor — Flagship Strategy (2026-06-16)

From the `touch-editor-flagship-research` workflow (8 agents, ~867k tokens) + a code-grounded audit of our editor (BuilderShell, VariantPicker, PropertiesPanel/StyleableField, EXTERNAL_HTML shim, ai.service generateTouchTemplate).

## Verdict
We are closer to best-in-class than the team probably thinks — and further from "flagship" than the demo suggests. The bones are genuinely strong and, in two places, ahead of Canva: (1) our Touch Template Generator already does the hard architectural thing the whole industry is racing toward — schema-anchored, constrained generation that emits VALIDATED zone/widget JSON against a hard widget allowlist (ai.service.ts generateTouchTemplateInner), which is exactly the "Puck AI / editable-by-construction" pattern Canva had to BUILD A NEW FOUNDATIONAL MODEL to achieve. Our output is editable on day one; theirs was flat images until late 2025. (2) We own live operational data (POS auto-86, per-location pricing, scores, schedules, weather) and the integration plumbing — a moat no design tool (Canva/Figma/Adobe) and few signage tools (only OptiSync/OptiDev approach it) can match. The brand scraper (branding-scraper.service.ts) already extracts logos+palette+fonts WITH WCAG grading — most signage rivals don't.

But against the real bar we are a generation-1 AI editor in a generation-2 market. The flagship interaction in 2026 is NOT "type a prompt, get a template" (we have that) — it is (a) chat-to-edit on the live board ("make the header bigger, use our brand blue, move the menu left"), (b) return 3+ candidates not 1, (c) one-click magic-resize across screen shapes, and (d) proactive, domain-aware suggestions the operator never asked for. We do ZERO of those four. We also have no persisted brand VOICE (only visual brand), no AI on the EXTERNAL_HTML click-to-edit shim (the shim reports the clicked element but no AI acts on it), and the Integration Concierge auto-seed loop is vision, not shipped. Editor mechanics still trail Yodeck/OptiSigns on table-stakes (smart guides, snap, rotate handle, layers/lock) — our own tracked Canva-parity gaps. Net: B+ engine, C+ experience. The good news is that nearly every flagship move REUSES infrastructure we already shipped, so the gap is months of focused work, not a rebuild.

## Flagship vision — "it almost makes itself"
A non-technical K-12 office manager or a venue ops lead sits down with an iPad. They tap "New board," and instead of a blank canvas they see a single text box: "Tell me what you want, or paste your website." They type one sentence — "a lunch menu board with today's specials and a countdown to Friday's game" — OR they paste their school URL. Within ~8 seconds three finished, on-brand boards fan out as large tappable cards: their logo is already there, their colors and fonts are applied (pulled live from the site), the copy reads in their voice ("Wildcat Cafeteria" not "Cafeteria"), and the lunch zone is already WIRED to the POS so today's actual prices are showing and a sold-out item is auto-86'd off the screen. They tap the one they like. It opens full-screen, and every word, image, and color is tap-to-edit — no flat image, no "where do I click."

Now the part nobody else does: a small chat pill floats at the bottom. They talk to the board in plain English — "make the title bigger so we can read it from the hallway," "use our away-game red," "move the countdown to the top." Each change ripples in live, on the actual zones, and Cmd+Z (or a back arrow on iPad) undoes it like any edit. While they work, the editor gently nudges them, unprompted and domain-smart: "This headline is too small to read from 8 feet — bump it?", "You haven't set an emergency fallback for this screen," "We found your Instagram on your site — add a live feed?" — suggestions a designer-friend would make, things they never thought of.

When they're done, one button: "Use on every screen." The board reflows — not stretches — into the portrait kiosk by the front door, the 16:9 TVs in the gym, and the 320×1080 LED ribbon over the entrance: re-typeset, repositioned, focal-points preserved, each shape genuinely laid out. They review three thumbnails, tap approve, and it's live across the fleet. The whole time the AI never spent a dime of anyone's budget without telling them, and never shipped a board that would break on the Taurus LED wall or fail contrast. The feeling we are selling: "I described what I wanted in one sentence and talked to it like a person, and it built something better than I could have — it almost made itself."

## AI build-it architecture (mapped to our 3-tier cost model; reuses generateTouchTemplate, Concierge, sparkle, variants-register, EXTERNAL_HTML shim)
- PROMPT -> 3 CANDIDATES (not 1). REUSE generateTouchTemplate's schema-anchored emit + vertical voice clause; net-new = loop the call 3x with diversified system-prompt seeds (different layout density/theme) and return an array. UI returns a grid of 3 ScaledTemplateThumbnail cards. TIER 1 (platform Concierge, setup-time) for the first generation in onboarding; TIER 2 (BYOK) for ongoing 'generate' once a key is set. Effort is mostly fan-out + thumbnail grid; the dangerous part (validated, Taurus-safe, brand-applied output) is already solved.
- CHAT-TO-EDIT AGENT over the open template (the flagship, critical-gap item). Net-new agent endpoint that receives the current template's zone/widget JSON + the NL instruction, and returns a DIFF of field mutations against the SAME StyleableField/PropertiesPanel contract the editor already renders. Apply diffs as normal undoable edits. Two response modes (Adobe pattern): inline-preview-then-apply for ambiguous asks ('make it better'), apply-then-confirm for unambiguous ('make the title blue'). Reuse: zone/widget schema, brand tokens (var(--brand-primary)/accent), the failure-cap + provider-resolution wrappers, Haiku for fast edit-resolution. TIER 2 BYOK (everyday creative). For EXTERNAL_HTML boards, drive it THROUGH the existing educms-field-click shim contract: clicked element -> AI rewrites that field -> apply via the existing inbound override channel (no React-into-iframe needed). This makes the 107 HTML boards + menu boards AI-editable with the plumbing already shipped.
- BRAND-EXTRACT + BRAND VOICE from URL/logo. REUSE branding-scraper.service.ts (already extracts logos, ranked colors, derived palette, Google-font match, WCAG grades). Net-new: (a) persist a per-tenant brandVoice string (Prisma field) and a small AI pass that infers tone from scraped homepage copy (Canva PDF-voice pattern); (b) feed brandVoice into EVERY generate/chat call so copy is on-brand by default — replacing today's hardcoded per-vertical voice clauses with tenant-specific voice layered on top. TIER 1 (setup-time, platform key) — this is the Integration Concierge's brand step.
- MAGIC-RESIZE / REFLOW across screen shapes (critical gap, biggest leapfrog). REUSE: screen resolution/orientation/N-panel model (#122/#123), per-orientation portrait widgets, the transform:scale wrapper as the floor. Net-new: an AI reflow pass that takes the source zone-graph + a target canvas (16:9 / portrait / 320×1080 ribbon / kiosk / N-panel) and RE-LAYS-OUT zones (reposition + re-typeset + focal-point-aware image crop) rather than scaling — emitting a per-target zone variant the renderer already understands. Must run output through the existing taurus-safety + auto-fit-floor + WCAG gates so every variant is provably device-safe. ALWAYS show the operator the per-target thumbnails for a one-tap human-review step (Canva/Adobe both admit reflow needs cleanup). TIER 2 BYOK.
- REAL-DATA AUTOFILL (bind + write). REUSE: data-source/fetch (json/csv), POS auto-86 + per-location pricing plumbing, menu-board live engines. Net-new: a 'bind any list/grid widget to a data source' designer action (OptiSync Repeater pattern) + an AI step that, at generate-time, both WIRES the binding (operator's connected POS/sheet/feed) AND writes the surrounding copy from the feed ('today's specials' blurbs from POS items). Surface a 'LIVE' badge on bound fields. This is our deepest moat (Canva/Figma can't touch live ops data). TIER 1 to propose+wire during Concierge; TIER 2 for ongoing copy generation.
- PROACTIVE SUGGESTIONS engine (unclaimed in signage — pure leapfrog). Net-new but mostly DETERMINISTIC, not LLM (keeps cost ~0): an in-editor rule set that lints the open board against domain knowledge — tap targets <44px, text too small for 8-ft viewing, contrast < WCAG AA on brand palette, no emergency fallback set on the screen, menu not wired to POS, logo missing (but found on their site), no idle/attract reset on a touch template, gestures requiring pinch/drag. Surface as one-tap-fix chips (Adobe inline pattern). LLM only for the optional 'idea' suggestions ('add a countdown to Friday's game') — TIER 1, capped. The accessibility/Taurus/8-ft checks are FREE and uniquely ours because we already encode those constraints in CI.
- BUILD-IT-FOR-ME / INTEGRATION CONCIERGE merge (the 'it almost makes itself' front door). REUSE: Integration Concierge discovery vision + Touch Template Generator. Net-new orchestration that fuses them: 'I run a pizza shop' -> discover POS (Toast/Square) -> wire it -> generate 3 menu boards already bound to live price + auto-86 -> auto-seed template #1. This is the agentic goal->design front door (Canva AI 2.0 pattern) but with INTEGRATION orchestration Canva can't do. TIER 1 (platform-funded, setup-time, hard-capped).
- PHOTO/SKETCH -> EDITABLE BOARD (on-ramp wedge). Net-new vision pass (cost-watch): operator photographs a paper menu / foamcore sign / competitor TV -> vision model maps it to our widget registry (LUNCH_MENU, IMAGE, TEXT) -> returns an editable VenueOS template grounded in the allowlist (NOT a flat image). Reuse the same constrained-emit + Taurus/contrast gates as generateTouchTemplate. TIER 1 at onboarding (highest-empathy moment), watch vision cost; cap hard.
- ECONOMIC-MODEL GUARDRAIL (cross-cutting, already mandated). Every flow above honors the 3-tier split: setup-time generation (3-candidate first board, brand+voice extract, concierge wiring, photo-import) on the PLATFORM key with lifetime+daily caps; everyday creative (chat-to-edit, magic-resize, ongoing copy) on TENANT BYOK; NEVER silently spend platform budget on a BYOK action — when no key, say 'Configure your AI provider' (already enforced in ai.service.ts). Reuse the existing windowCount/recordEvent caps + provider resolution wholesale.

## Quick wins
- 3-candidate output: loop the existing generateTouchTemplate fan-out 3x with diversified seeds and show a pick-the-winner grid — the schema-safe emit, brand-apply, and Taurus gates are already done, so this is mostly UI. Single highest demo-impact-per-day move.
- Persist + use brand voice: add a tenant brandVoice field, infer it once from scraped homepage copy, and prepend it to every generate/chat call — turns generic copy on-brand for ~1 day of work.
- Deterministic proactive-suggestion chips: lint the open board against rules we ALREADY encode (8-ft font floor, WCAG contrast on brand palette, tap-target <44px, no emergency fallback, logo found on site) — zero LLM cost, unclaimed in the category, instant 'it nudges me' wow.
- Sparkle -> inline tone chips: upgrade the existing sparkle copy button to offer Rewrite / Shorter / More formal / Translate inline on the clicked element (Magic Write pattern) — small change to a shipped feature, reuses the click-to-edit hot-zone that already reports the element.
- 'Brand this board' one-tap recolor button: we already have the brand-apply plumbing — surface it as a first-class one-tap action with a shuffle-palette toggle on every template.
- Snap-to-grid/center/margins with Cmd-to-bypass: the cheapest mechanic that makes non-designer layouts look intentional; closes a tracked Canva-parity gap and is contained to the builder canvas.

## Big bets
- Chat-to-edit agent across ALL THREE editing architectures (React-zone, EXTERNAL_HTML shim, holiday bridge) driven by one conversational layer — harder than any single-format competitor faces, and a genuine moat once shipped. Start React-zone, then ride the existing educms-field-click shim contract to the 107 HTML boards.
- AI magic-resize/reflow for REAL signage hardware shapes (LED ribbon 320×1080, N-panel daisy-chain, portrait kiosk, 16:9) with Taurus-safety + contrast guaranteed by construction — a category no consumer tool will ever enter and our most defensible differentiator versus Canva's social-aspect-ratio resize.
- Build-it-for-me Integration Concierge front door: 'describe your business' -> discover + wire the POS/streaming/scheduling stack -> generate 3 boards already bound to live data + auto-86 -> seed template #1. The end-to-end 'it builds itself INCLUDING the plumbing' moment that Canva/Figma/Adobe structurally cannot do because they don't own the integrations or the live data.
- Constraint-based auto-layout engine (Figma Hug/Fill + min/max) replacing imperative scale math — the structural cure for our recurring overlap/auto-fit fires and the right substrate for reliable reflow and generation, but a deep rebuild to sequence last and behind a flag.

## Risks
- Flat/uneditable output is the #1 trust-killer in the category — but it is the ONE trap we've already avoided (schema-anchored emit). The risk is REGRESSING it: every new AI flow (reflow, photo-import, chat-to-edit) must emit into the validated zone/widget schema or run through the same Taurus/contrast/auto-fit gates, never raw HTML/CSS. Raw AI-emitted markup could ship inset/gap/backdrop-filter that dies on Chromium-83 or minified JS that throws in WebKit — both documented multi-day fires here.
- Cost blowout: 3-candidate generation, chat-to-edit, reflow, and vision (photo-import) multiply spend fast. The 3-tier model is correct but must be ENFORCED — setup-time on platform key with hard caps, everyday creative on BYOK, never silently spend platform budget on a BYOK action. Watch vision cost especially.
- Latency breaks the magic: OptiSigns markets <30s; a 30-90s spinner on chat-to-edit feels broken. Use Haiku for edit-resolution (small local diffs), stream/optimistic-apply, reserve bigger models for full synthesis only.
- Brand drift across many outputs: LLMs lose brand consistency over dozens of generations. Inject the brand kit + voice into every call AND add a post-gen brand-check that flags off-palette/off-font results (MS Copilot pattern) before they ship to screens.
- Over-claiming 'done': this codebase's own history (the logo bug round 7) and the industry pattern is shipping AI buttons that don't work end-to-end. Verify the FULL generate -> edit -> resize -> schedule -> render-on-real-screen loop (and on WebKit + Taurus) before any flagship claim — Verification-Before-Claim applies harder to AI than anything else.
- Reflow imperfection: even Canva/Adobe admit auto-resize needs cleanup. Never blind-auto-emit across shapes — always force a per-target human-review thumbnail step with snap-to-grid cleanup, or operators get broken boards live on the fleet.
- Mechanics gap undercuts the AI lead: if smart guides/snap/rotate/layers stay missing, demos feel less polished than Yodeck/OptiSigns no matter how good the AI is — close the table-stakes parity in parallel, not after.

## Adversarial critique (principal-engineer pass)
**Build first:** Build the deterministic, zero-LLM "proactive suggestion chips" engine FIRST (the plan's P0 #3) — but scope v1 tightly to the checks the codebase ALREADY encodes in CI: 8-ft font floor, WCAG contrast on the brand palette, tap-target <44px, no emergency-fallback set on the screen, logo-found-on-site-but-missing, Taurus inset/gap landmines, and below-50px auto-fit floor. Surface each as a one-tap-fix chip wired to the existing updateZone(id, patch, commit:true) mutation.
**Why:** It is the highest leverage-to-risk ratio move on the board, for four concrete reasons grounded in this codebase. (1) ZERO marginal cost/latency/provider risk — it's pure deterministic rules, so it sidesteps every one of the plan's own top risks (cost blowout, latency-breaks-magic, brand-drift, flat-output regression) while still delivering the 'it nudges me like a designer-friend' wow that is genuinely unclaimed in the entire signage category. (2) It is uniquely OURS and structurally impossible for Canva/Figma/Adobe to copy, because the rules ARE our hard constraints — Chromium-83 Taurus safety, 8-ft legibility, emergency-fallback presence, WCAG-on-brand — which no consumer design tool encodes. (3) It is the lowest-risk way to PROVE the apply-mutation contract (updateZone + commit + the existing past/future history rail) end-to-end on a one-tap-fix BEFORE betting the flagship chat-to-edit agent on that same contract; chat-to-edit (the plan's true flagship) is effort-L and rides entirely on this mutation+undo path working, so validating it via a deterministic chip first is the correct de-risking sequence the plan implies but never states. (4) It directly serves the operator the whole product is built around — a non-IT office manager who will ship an illegible or contrast-failing board to a hallway screen — and it enforces the Verification-Before-Claim and 30-second-happy-path acceptance gates the project already mandates. The plan ranks 3-candidate generation as the 'highest demo-impact-per-day' P0; that's a better DEMO but a worse FIRST BUILD, because it 3x's platform AI spend on day one and proves none of the editing plumbing the flagship depends on.

**Missed capabilities**
- MULTIPLAYER / CONCURRENT CO-EDITING + COMMENTS. Canva, Figma, and Adobe Express all ship real-time co-editing and comment threads. The builder folder literally contains D6_MULTIPLAYER_DEFERRED.md — it was consciously deferred but the gap matrix never lists it. For DISTRICT buyers (multiple office staff on one board) and multi-location operators (Domino's pilot, 50-location menu rollouts), 'two people can't edit the same board / leave a comment for approval' is a deal-shaped hole the plan omits entirely. It is also the natural partner to the RBAC/brand-lock bullet.
- TEMPLATE/BRAND-KIT MARKETPLACE + STARTER-GALLERY DEPTH. Canva/Yodeck/OptiSigns lead with thousands of pro starting templates + a marketplace. For a non-technical operator, 'start from a great template and tweak' beats 'generate from a prompt' for trust and speed. The plan treats generation as the only on-ramp and never grades catalog breadth/quality or a community/marketplace motion — a real best-in-class axis it skips.
- ANIMATION / MOTION / TRANSITION AUTHORING in the editor. BrightSign/OptiSigns/Yodeck and Canva/Adobe let operators add per-element entrance/loop/transition motion. The org clearly CAN do cinematic motion (celebration decks), but the matrix never asks whether the TEMPLATE EDITOR exposes motion authoring to operators — a visible polish gap on a 'flagship' claim.
- AI IMAGE GENERATION + GENERATIVE FILL/EXPAND + BACKGROUND REMOVAL + STOCK/ICON LIBRARY. The plan lists photo->board (input) but omits AI image GENERATION, generative background expand, background removal, and an integrated stock/icon library — all of which Canva/Adobe ship and operators expect when a board needs a hero image. CLAUDE.md's own Standard Audit Surface §4 explicitly enumerates these as audit targets; the plan silently drops them.
- TEXT-TO-SPEECH / MULTILINGUAL TRANSLATION of generated boards. CLAUDE.md §4 lists 'AI translation for multilingual templates' and 'TTS for emergency announcements' as standing audit targets, and multilingual signage (ES/EN) is table-stakes for K-12 and QSR. The plan's brand-voice work is monolingual-English-only and never touches one-click translate or accessibility TTS — a competitive and a11y gap.
- THE BUILDER'S EXISTING UNDO/HISTORY RAIL AS REQUIRED PLUMBING. useBuilderStore.ts already ships a full past/future snapshot history with HISTORY_LIMIT and undo()/redo(), and the canonical mutation API is updateZone(id, patch, commit?) / updateZones(...) where the `commit` boolean is exactly the history-commit hook. Chat-to-edit MUST funnel its diffs through these same commit points or undo silently breaks — the plan asserts 'apply as normal undoable edits' without naming this contract, which is the single most error-prone integration point and the thing that makes or breaks the flagship feature.
- SERVER-BACKED DURABLE UNDO PATTERN ALREADY PROVEN IN SPORTS (task T1-2: 'Durable 10-deep undo rail via GameEvent'). The team already solved durable, server-persisted, multi-actor undo for the sports console. Chat-to-edit on templates is the same shape (AI is just another actor mutating shared state) yet the plan proposes a net-new agent without reusing or even referencing this proven pattern — a missed reuse that also de-risks the multiplayer gap above.

**Mis-prioritized**

**Constraint blind-spots**
- WRONG ON THE EDITOR-MECHANICS GAP — the single biggest factual error. The plan rates smart-guides/snap/rotate/layers/lock as 'PARTIAL, tracked Canva-parity gaps still open' and assigns a P0 effort-L sweep. The code shows ALL of these already shipped and mounted: snap-engine.ts (snapMove/snapResize), active snap-LINES with human-readable labels (Center/Edge/percent), snapEnabled + showGrid + showGuides toggles in BuilderCanvas, a mounted LayersPanel with per-zone lock/unlock (zone.locked field in types.ts), and zone rotation+opacity (transform:rotate in BuilderZone). This P0-L is largely already done; keeping it as a flagship blocker mis-spends a top slot. The real remaining mechanic is a DRAG rotate-HANDLE (rotation is numeric-only today) — a small task, not effort-L.
- THE EXTERNAL_HTML 'inbound override channel' IS NOT A LIVE FIELD-MUTATION CHANNEL — it's a coarse URL-param re-render. PropertiesPanel applies overrides to the sandboxed null-origin iframe via ?brand=/?text= URL params that force the board to re-fetch+re-parse; the code comment EXPLICITLY rejects a postMessage bridge for inbound apply due to a documented race condition (panel mounts before iframe). The shim's postMessage is OUTBOUND-only (educms-field-click reporting which element was clicked) plus educms-edit-mode arming. So the plan's claim that chat-to-edit on the 107 HTML boards is 'no React-into-iframe needed, apply via the existing inbound override channel' understates the work: per-field live AI mutation needs either a new inbound postMessage apply path (which the team deliberately avoided) or a full ?param re-render per edit (clunky, full-board reload per word change). This is real architecture, not 'plumbing already shipped' — and it's why the plan correctly sequences it P1, but for the wrong stated reason.
- LATENCY + STREAMING UX FOR CHAT-TO-EDIT IS UNDER-SPECIFIED AGAINST THE TARGET DEVICE. The flagship vision is an operator on an iPAD, and the plan's own risk section flags latency, but the architecture never commits to optimistic-apply + streamed diffs as a HARD requirement, nor accounts for the fact that the AI dispatch path is server-roundtrip + provider-roundtrip + JSON parse + sanitize before any mutation lands. On cellular iPad in a gym, a 'make the title blue' that takes 3-6s feels broken. The deterministic chips (build-first) have zero latency; chat-to-edit needs a spec'd optimistic-apply-then-reconcile loop, not just 'use Haiku.'
- MAGIC-RESIZE/REFLOW IGNORES THAT ~107 FLAGSHIP BOARDS ARE EXTERNAL_HTML AT FIXED 3840×2160 / 1920×1080, NOT REACT ZONES. The reflow architecture assumes the source is a re-layoutable zone-graph. But the best-looking, most-shipped boards (hs/kiosk/signage/fitness) are self-contained fixed-canvas HTML documents the renderer can only transform:scale — it CANNOT re-typeset or reposition their internal elements. So 'AI reflow across shapes' realistically applies ONLY to React-zone templates; for the EXTERNAL_HTML catalog it degrades to letterbox/scale (the very thing the plan says it's replacing). The XL effort estimate is right but the COVERAGE is overstated — the wildest shapes (LED ribbon, N-panel) are exactly where the fixed-canvas HTML boards can't reflow.
- MULTI-VERTICAL VOICE IS TREATED AS A SOLVED REUSE WHEN IT'S A KNOWN-BRITTLE STRING TABLE. brandVoice is proposed as 'one field + one inference pass,' but VERTICAL_VOICE today is a hardcoded per-vertical clause map and the touch-template path only applies voice to PLACEHOLDER copy in TEXT/ANNOUNCEMENT/QUOTE zones. Layering a per-tenant voice on top across 11+ verticals, in multiple languages, with a post-gen brand-CHECK to catch off-voice output (which the plan lists as a risk but not as work) is more than 'S effort.' Underestimating it risks shipping 'AI slop' — the exact failure the bullet is meant to prevent.
