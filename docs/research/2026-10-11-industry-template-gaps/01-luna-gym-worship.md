- **Priority 1: Club policies and safety orientation.** The reviewed preset descriptions and source set cover occupancy, classes, equipment, amenities, trainer and member information, but I found no dedicated passive board with editable facility rules, age/guest guidance, equipment etiquette, or where to get help. Nearest sources are `fitness-lobby-welcome` and `preset-sig-gym-01`; neither is described as a policy board. A new board should expose policy copy and contact/help location as separate fields so operators can update rules without replacing a class or welcome layout.
- **Priority 2: Facility hours and holiday/exception notices.** I found no dedicated passive workflow for opening hours or closures/modified hours. The nearest sources are `fitness-lobby-welcome` and the gym lobby/kiosk workflows, but their described fields center on classes, promotions, capacity, amenities and member info. A compact hours/exception board should distinguish regular hours from dated exceptions and offer an optional image/logo slot.
