# Directory art direction — October 25, 2026

These four replacements respond to a failed aesthetic review of the original gallery. Technical fit checks were insufficient: repeating a left rail and card grid in new colors did not create distinct environments. The rebuilt designs use architecture, material, scale and information hierarchy. No oversized arrows, generic monograms or botanical clip art remain in these four scopes.

## Primary studio references

- [THERE — The Frederick](https://therestudio.com/work/the-frederick-mirvac-precinct-brand-identity-and-campaign/): the studio presents residential branding as a coordinated system of naming, identity, art direction, photography and spatial applications. Our interpretation is to make the residence's architecture central to its identity, while keeping the destination index restrained. We did not copy the brand or its imagery.
- [Entro — Residential community wayfinding](https://entro.com/wayfinding/residential-community/): residential signs should convey the particular development's character and help residents and unfamiliar visitors. Our interpretation is to connect materials and landscape imagery to calm, highly legible destination information.
- [Two Twelve — Comcast Innovation and Technology Center](https://www.twotwelve.com/project/comcast-innovation-and-technology-center): the studio describes a coordinated color and art program supporting workplace zoning. Our interpretation is to give the creative-campus index a strong architectural identity and prominent suite coordinates. Color remains supplementary; names and numbers carry the information.
- [THERE — Portfolio](https://therestudio.com/): the studio's hotel, workplace and property work reinforces the need for a specific identity for each environment. This is a design interpretation, not an endorsement or claim that these studios produced this package.
