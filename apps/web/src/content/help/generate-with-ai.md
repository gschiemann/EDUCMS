# Designing with AI

VenueOS can design complete, on-brand boards from a one-sentence description — and then edit them the same way. This guide covers turning AI on, generating your first board, and refining it.

## Turn AI on (one time, admin only)

AI runs on *your* AI account so you control the spend:

1. Open **Settings → AI provider → Configure**.
2. Pick a provider — **Anthropic (Claude)** is recommended; OpenAI and Google also work.
3. Paste an API key from that provider (each option shows exactly where to get one).
4. Choose a model tier — **Standard** for everyday copy, **Premium** for full board designs.

We test the key before saving, so a typo fails loudly here instead of silently later. If a teammate sees "AI isn't enabled yet," this step is what's missing.

## Generate a board

1. Open **Templates** and click **Generate with AI**.
2. Describe what you want, the way you'd tell a designer: *"a taco special board for Friday happy hour with our brand colors"*.
3. You get **three different drafts** — Balanced, Bold, and Detailed. Open any of them full-screen.
4. **Keep** the one you like. It lands in your template library, fully editable, and photo-appropriate boards come back with a real, relevant photo already art-directed behind legible text.

Nothing is published to a screen until you schedule it — generating is always safe to explore.

## Edit with words

Inside the builder, every AI-capable zone has an **Edit with words** box:

1. Type one change at a time — *"make the title bigger"*, *"use the brand color on the header"*. Short, single instructions work best.
2. Review the **proposed change** it shows you.
3. **Apply** or **Discard**. Undo (⌘Z) always restores the previous look if you change your mind.

## The sparkle button

Text fields across the builder have a ✨ button that writes or rewrites copy in context — announcement blurbs, menu descriptions, welcome messages. Same provider, same controls.

## Translate a whole board

**Translate** in the builder's top bar localizes every text field on the board in one pass — useful for multilingual campuses and menus.

## Costs and limits

- AI calls bill to **your provider key** — typical text generations cost fractions of a cent; full board designs cost a few cents each.
- Hourly caps are built in so a runaway session can't surprise you.
- No key configured? Every AI button says so honestly and the rest of VenueOS works exactly the same.
