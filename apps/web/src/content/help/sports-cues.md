# Fire celebrations and custom cues

A **cue** is a moment you trigger on the displays — a celebration animation, a sponsor takeover, a hype graphic.

## Fire a cue

1. On the **Run game** screen, tap **Cues**.
2. A popup opens on top of the game console — your cues are one tap away.
3. Tap a cue. It fires on the displays, the tile flashes, and the popup closes itself — you're straight back to the game.

You never leave the Run screen to fire a cue.

## Pick where it fires

At the top of the cue popup, choose the target:

- **Everywhere** — every screen showing this game.
- **Scoreboard** — scoreboards and broadcast overlays only.
- **Ribbon** — ribbon and fascia boards only.

## Built-in celebrations

Every sport ships with celebrations matched to it — Touchdown, Field Goal, and Sack for football; Three-pointer and Dunk for basketball; Home Run for baseball; and so on.

## Custom cues

Build your own from the cue popup with **New cue**: give it a name and choose its content (an uploaded image or a solid color). A custom cue can be a sponsor takeover, a "Make some noise" graphic, or a t-shirt-toss prompt.

The *look* of celebrations — overlay style and timing — is configured under **Set up**, before the game. Firing them happens here, on the Run screen.
