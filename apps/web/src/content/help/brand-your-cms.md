# Branding — make VenueOS yours

Your screens should look like *your* organization, not like software. Branding takes about a minute.

## Set it up

1. Open **Settings → Organization**. It opens on **Logo & colors**.
2. Press **Update branding**, then **paste your website address** and press **Scan**. We visit it and pull your logo, brand colors, and fonts automatically.
3. Review what we found — pick a different logo, upload your own, or adjust a color if you like.
4. Press **Adopt branding**.

No website yet? Upload your logo and choose your colors by hand in the same place.

## Where your brand shows up

- **The dashboard itself** — your logo and palette replace ours, so the tool feels in-house.
- **Templates** — every color picker in the builder offers **Brand primary** and **Brand accent** presets, and brand-aware presets adopt your palette.
- **AI designs** — boards you generate come back in your colors with your logo placed, not generic clip-art styling.

## Apply it to existing templates

Already built templates before branding? Use **Apply brand to templates** to sweep your palette across them in one pass — then spot-check each board, since hand-tuned colors are respected rather than bulldozed.

## Organizations with multiple locations

Each location can carry its own branding (a chain's stores, or a district's schools, each with their own logo and colors), or inherit the parent's — set it per location in that location's Settings.
