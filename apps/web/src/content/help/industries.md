# Industries

VenueOS speaks your language. A school sees hallway and cafeteria boards; a restaurant sees menu boards and combos; a gym sees class schedules and trainer welcomes. That's driven by your workspace's **industry**.

## What your industry changes

- **The template gallery** — every industry gets its own designed presets (schools get welcome and cafeteria boards; restaurants get menu, promo, and loyalty boards; gyms get class & training boards; sports venues get scoreboards and ribbon boards).
- **Categories and navigation** — the filters and menu items match how your business thinks.
- **Safety defaults** — schools start with **Emergency alerts armed** (lockdown, weather, evacuation are core to K-12); commercial venues start with them off and can enable them any time.
- **Sample content and AI tone** — starter content and AI-written copy match your world.

## Picking your industry

You choose it when creating your workspace. Not sure? Pick the closest — everything is changeable.

## Switching later

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find **Industry** and click **Switch**.
3. Pick the new one — it applies immediately: gallery, categories, and defaults follow on the spot.

Your custom templates, screens, playlists, and schedules are never touched by a switch — only the ready-made catalog and defaults change.

## Multiple locations

A parent workspace (a district, a franchise, a chain) can hold child locations, and each location keeps its own industry. A property group can run a gym, a restaurant, and an office lobby side by side — one login, the right experience at every location.
