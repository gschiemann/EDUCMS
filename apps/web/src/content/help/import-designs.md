# Importing a design you already have

If your team already designs in PowerPoint, Canva, Google Slides, or has PDFs from a print shop, you don't have to rebuild anything. Import the file, review what each page turns into, and add the pages you want.

## What you can import

- **PDF** — the universal path. Every page is rendered exactly as it looks, and its text is also pulled out, so you can choose per page which you want.
- **PowerPoint (.pptx)** — comes in as editable text and pictures. We can't yet redraw a slide exactly the way PowerPoint does, so if you want it pixel-perfect, use **File → Export → PDF** and import that instead.
- **Images** — PNG, JPG or WEBP. One image, one template.
- **A VenueOS template file (.json)** — exported from another account. It's restored as-is, scenes and all, with no conversion step.

Older `.ppt` files aren't supported. Open one in PowerPoint and **Save a Copy as .pptx**, or export it to PDF.

## How it works

1. Open **Templates** and click **Import design**.
2. Drop your file in. We convert it on our side first — nothing is added yet.
3. **Review.** Every page of your document is listed, with a picture of what it converted to. Tick the ones you want.
4. For each page, choose how it comes in:
   - **Keep the look** — the page exactly as it is, as a picture. Text inside it is part of the picture.
   - **Editable layers** — text and pictures become elements you can retype, restyle and move. The panel tells you how many of each that page will give you.
5. Click **Add**. The button says how many templates it will create, and that is how many you get.

## What you'll see flagged

The review step tells you anything that didn't come through cleanly, page by page, before you commit:

- A page with artwork but no text is marked **No text to edit**. It's still listed and can still be added as a picture — it is never dropped quietly.
- If your document is longer than we convert in one go, we say so and how many pages were rendered.
- Fonts we don't have, shapes we can't rebuild and pictures that failed to load are each named against the page they're on.
- Text that would have landed unreadable on its own background — dark on a dark slide, or white where we could not bring the picture background across — is recoloured so you can see it, and the page says so. Change it back in the builder if you'd rather.

Anything we could not convert at all is still listed, so you always know it wasn't missed.

## Making an imported page live

This is the part worth knowing. Once a page is in as **editable layers**, open it in the builder, select a text box, and use **Make it live**. The line someone retypes every week — today's date, the lunch menu, the bell schedule — becomes an element that keeps itself current. That's the difference between a picture on a screen and a board that maintains itself.

## Tips

- Export at high resolution. 4K exports stay crisp on big displays.
- Match your screen's shape: 16:9 for landscape TVs, 9:16 for portrait displays.
- Animations, slide transitions and embedded video are not part of a conversion. If a deck depends on motion, export it to video and upload that as an asset instead.
- Importing doesn't put anything on a screen. Schedule the template when you're ready.
